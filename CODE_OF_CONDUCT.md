# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors must communicate with respect, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and keep technical disagreement focused on the work. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository owner through the project's established communication channel. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or ban participants when necessary to protect the community.

This policy applies in repository discussions, pull requests, issues, reviews, and project-related community spaces.
